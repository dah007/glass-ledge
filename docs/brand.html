NameStatus

Avoid "Drift…" names: Driftwell is a floating file shelf for Mac. Write the name as GlassLedge (capital G and L, no space).

Voice and taglines

  • Plain, warm, a little playful. Say what it does, not what it "empowers".
  • Your favorite folders, floating on your desktop. (primary)
  • Glass folders for your Windows 11 desktop.
  • Glance. Click. Open.
