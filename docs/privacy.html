Privacy policy

GlassLedge collects no personal information and sends nothing off your PC.

Effective 25 November 2026. This policy covers GlassLedge, the free, open-source Windows app made by dah007, however you install it (Microsoft Store or the installer on GitHub).

What GlassLedge does with your data

Nothing leaves your computer. GlassLedge has no accounts, no analytics or telemetry, no advertising, and makes no network connections of its own. It works fully offline.

What stays on your PC

  • Your settings file. For each widget, GlassLedge saves the folder you chose (or which plugin it shows), where the widget sits, its size, whether it is collapsed, the name you gave it, its color, and whether it is always on top. The order widgets are stacked in and a few app-wide choices (such as "Hide all" and your Preferences) are saved too. The file is settings.json in GlassLedge's own folder under your user profile. Only you and apps on your account can read it. Delete the folder, or uninstall, to remove it.
  • The contents of the folders you pin. To draw a widget, GlassLedge reads the names and icons of the files in that folder. It only reads them to show them on your screen. It does not copy, store or send them.
  • Your wallpaper's average color. GlassLedge reads your desktop wallpaper locally to pick text that stays readable. It keeps only one average color, in memory.

Links that open your browser

The About window has links to the author's GitHub, LinkedIn and Buy Me a Coffee pages. They open in your default web browser only when you click them. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and GlassLedge shares nothing with them.

Plugins and the Search box

Plugins are off until you turn on Allow plugins in Preferences. The Search box plugin that comes with GlassLedge sends nothing itself: when you press Search, it opens your default web browser with what you typed, and the browser sends it to the search engine under that search engine's privacy policy.

A plugin you add yourself is a separate program written by someone else. It runs with the same access as GlassLedge and is covered by its author's privacy policy, not this one. Only add plugins you trust.

The Microsoft Store

If you get GlassLedge from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect download and usage information about the Store itself under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. That is separate from GlassLedge, which receives none of it.

Children

GlassLedge collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If GlassLedge ever starts collecting or sending data, this page will be updated first and the date above changed. Earlier versions of this page are in the GitHub history.

Contact
